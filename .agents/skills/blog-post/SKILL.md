# Blog Post, Outbound & Pitch Authoring Skill

Before authoring, revising, or reviewing a public blog post (`learn/blog/*.md` + manual registration in `apps/portal/resources/data/blog.json` — SEO surfaces regenerate, never hand-edited) or pitch copy (`funding/**`), read and adhere to `.agents/skills/blog-post/references/blog-authoring-guide.md`.
For external venue/directory discovery, an outbound post/submission, `link-reply`, or `reply` (X, LinkedIn, Medium, newsletters, HN, directories), read and adhere to [outbound-post.md](./references/outbound-post.md) (`.agents/skills/blog-post/references/outbound-post.md`).

Every post or pitch is held to its thesis: reader/acceptance pre-flight, mining the record first, a diagram or image per blog post, one consolidated fact check at the final version (never per-revision V-B-A, #262), zero over-claims, and cross-family review before shipping (#13486).
