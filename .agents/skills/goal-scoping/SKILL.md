# Goal-Scoping — the planning front-end (goal → owned lanes)

If you are about to decompose a GOAL (a release, initiative, or major objective) into work — or you notice work being chased as scattered micro-tickets with no owned lanes, or you are tempted to skip planning or assign peers to work — you MUST immediately use the `view_file` tool to read and strictly adhere to `.agents/skills/goal-scoping/references/goal-scoping-workflow.md` before scoping or assigning anything.
## What it enforces
Planning front-end of the epic lifecycle: converts goals into a few coherent owned lanes (not scrap tickets); peers self-select lanes, and the planner defines goal+lanes without assigning peers.
