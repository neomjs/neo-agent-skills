# Ticket Triage Skill

If you are a maintainer-permission (`WRITE` or higher) agent encountering a ticket without proper labels, you MUST immediately use the `view_file` tool to read and strictly adhere to `.agents/skills/ticket-triage/references/ticket-triage-workflow.md` before applying any labels or making intake decisions.
## What it enforces
Codifies the social contract when a ticket arrives without `ai`, primary (`bug`/`enhancement`/`epic`), or secondary labels — typically authored by a non-maintainer contributor or lower-privileged agent who could not apply labels at create-time.
